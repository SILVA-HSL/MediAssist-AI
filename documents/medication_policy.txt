MEDICATION POLICY

All medications must be prescribed by an authorized medical practitioner.

Before administering medication, healthcare staff must verify the patient's identity using at least two patient identifiers.

Healthcare staff must check the medication name, dosage, route of administration, and expiration date before administration.

Any patient with a known drug allergy must have the allergy clearly documented in their medical records.

If a patient experiences an adverse reaction to medication, the medication administration must be stopped immediately and the responsible medical officer must be notified.

Controlled medications must be stored securely and access must be restricted to authorized healthcare personnel.

All administered medications must be accurately recorded in the patient's medical records.
